# Billing

## Billing Issues

Common billing issues include: - Unexpected charges - Duplicate
charges - Incorrect invoice amounts - Failed payments - Questions about
payment status

## Unexpected Charges

Before responding, verify the available billing information.

Do not assume that an unfamiliar charge is fraudulent or incorrectly
billed without supporting evidence.

If the charge cannot be explained using available information, escalate
the case for human review.

## Duplicate Charges

If two charges appear to represent the same transaction, collect the
relevant transaction details and escalate when the duplication cannot be
verified automatically.

## Failed Payments

A failed payment may prevent access to paid services.

Support should explain the available payment information and avoid
claiming that a payment succeeded unless the evidence confirms it.

## Billing Changes

Do not promise changes to invoices, charges, payment methods, or billing
status unless the available evidence and authorized company process
support the action.

## Sensitive Information

Never request or expose a customer's full payment-card number, security
code, password, or other unnecessary sensitive credentials.
