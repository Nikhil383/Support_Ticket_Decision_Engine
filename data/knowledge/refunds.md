# Refunds

## Refund Eligibility

Customers can request a refund for an eligible purchase within 30 days
of the original transaction.

Refunds are generally available when: - The customer was charged
incorrectly. - The customer was charged for a duplicate transaction. - A
service was purchased but could not be delivered. - The customer is
otherwise covered by an applicable refund policy.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

After a refund is approved, processing time depends on the payment
provider. Customers should allow several business days for the amount to
appear in their account.

## Refund Requests

Support should collect: - Customer account or order identifier -
Transaction date - Amount charged - Reason for the refund

Do not promise an exact refund arrival date unless the available account
or payment information confirms it.

## Exceptions

If the available information does not establish that a refund is
eligible, escalate the request for human review rather than inventing an
exception or approval.
