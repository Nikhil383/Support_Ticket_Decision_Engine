# Subscriptions

## Subscription Management

Common subscription requests include: - Starting a subscription -
Changing a subscription - Cancelling a subscription - Understanding
subscription status - Questions about renewal

## Cancellation

A cancellation request should be handled according to the available
subscription information and authorized process.

Do not claim that a subscription has been cancelled unless the available
evidence confirms the cancellation.

## Renewal

Customers may ask about renewal dates, renewal charges, or subscription
status.

Only provide a specific renewal date or charge when the available
information supports it.

## Plan Changes

When a customer requests a plan change, explain the available options
using the supplied knowledge.

Do not invent plan names, prices, discounts, features, or eligibility
requirements.

## Subscription Access

If a customer reports losing access to a subscribed service, first
distinguish between: - Subscription status - Billing/payment status -
Technical access problems

If the available evidence cannot establish the cause, escalate for
further investigation.

## Human Review

Requests involving unclear subscription status, disputed charges,
exceptions, or actions that cannot be verified from available
information should be referred for human review.
