# Subscription Support Knowledge Base

## Cancellation

Check subscription status and whether
cancellation is immediate or scheduled for
the end of the billing period.

Never claim cancellation is complete until
the system confirms it.

## Upgrade or Downgrade

Verify:

- Current plan
- Requested plan
- Pricing
- Effective date
- Proration rules

## Failed Renewal

Check the latest invoice, payment state,
and subscription status before saying the
subscription has ended.

## Free Trial

Verify:

- Trial dates
- Payment requirements
- Conversion behavior
- Cancellation rules
