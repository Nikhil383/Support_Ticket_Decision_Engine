# Billing Support Knowledge Base

## Duplicate Charge

Verify:

- Transaction IDs
- Amounts
- Timestamps
- Invoice information

Determine whether both entries are completed
charges or one is a temporary authorization.

Never confirm a refund without billing-system
confirmation.

## Failed Payment

Check invoice and payment status before
claiming an account or subscription has
been cancelled.

## Refund

Verify the applicable refund policy and
transaction state.

Never claim a refund is complete without
confirmation.

## Security

Never request:

- Full card numbers
- CVVs
- Passwords
- Authentication secrets
