# Refund Support Knowledge Base

## Refund Request

Verify the transaction and applicable refund
policy before communicating eligibility.

## Duplicate Charge

Verify that two completed charges exist
before describing a charge as duplicated.

## Refund Confirmation

Never tell a customer that a refund has
been completed unless the billing system
confirms the refund status.

## Escalation

Escalate unclear, disputed, or unsupported
refund cases to a human billing agent.
